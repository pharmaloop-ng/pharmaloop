# Problem Statement

Pharmacy systems in many emerging markets operate under structural inefficiencies that directly impact public health outcomes.

---

## Key Problems

### 1. Weak Inventory Visibility
Most pharmacies lack real-time insight into:
- Stock levels
- Expiry timelines
- Demand patterns

---

### 2. Climate Exposure Risks
High temperatures and unstable storage conditions:
- Reduce medicine effectiveness
- Increase wastage
- Create hidden quality risks

---

### 3. Fragmented Supply Chains
Distributors, pharmacies, and manufacturers often operate in isolation, leading to:
- Stockouts
- Over-ordering
- Inefficient distribution cycles

---

### 4. Compliance Burden
Regulatory compliance is often manual and reactive, making it difficult for pharmacies to maintain consistent standards.

---

## Impact

- Reduced patient access to essential medicines
- Financial losses for pharmacy operators
- Weak data for healthcare planning and investment decisions
