# Project Overview — Pharmaloop

Pharmaloop is a pharmacy intelligence and infrastructure platform designed to modernize how pharmacies operate in climate-sensitive and resource-constrained environments.

It combines operational management tools with climate-aware insights to help pharmacies maintain medicine quality, reduce losses, and improve service delivery - with particular attention to essential medicines for children and vulnerable populations in climate-exposed communities.

---

## Context

In many regions, particularly across Nigeria and similar climates:

- High temperatures affect medicine stability
- Supply chains are fragmented
- Inventory systems are often manual or outdated
- Regulatory compliance is inconsistent due to operational pressure

Pharmaloop is designed to solve these challenges through technology-driven coordination and intelligence.

---

## What Pharmaloop Does

Pharmaloop provides:

1. Digital pharmacy operations management
2. Inventory tracking and forecasting
3. Climate-aware storage insights
4. Supplier and distributor coordination tools
5. Compliance tracking and documentation support

---

## Users

- Retail/Community pharmacy owners
- Pharmacy chains
- Pharmaceutical distributors
- Healthcare investors
- Regulatory stakeholders (indirectly)

---

## Outcome

Better managed pharmacies, reduced medicine waste, improved availability of essential drugs, and more resilient healthcare delivery systems.
