# Regulatory Considerations (Nigeria Context)

Pharmaloop is designed to support, not replace, regulatory compliance in pharmaceutical operations.

---

## Regulatory Environment

Pharmacy operations in Nigeria are influenced by:
- Pharmacists Council of Nigeria (PCN)
- National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC) regulations
- Federal and state health policies

---

## Key Compliance Areas

### 1. Licensing
Pharmacies must maintain valid operational licenses and pharmacist supervision.

---

### 2. Storage Standards
Regulations require proper storage of medicines, including:
- Temperature control for sensitive drugs
- Protection from heat and humidity

---

### 3. Record Keeping
Pharmacies must maintain:
- Inventory logs
- Dispensing records
- Supplier documentation

---

### 4. Controlled Substances
Strict tracking and documentation required for regulated medicines.

---

## How Pharmaloop Supports Compliance

Pharmaloop is designed to:
- Digitize record keeping
- Improve audit readiness
- Support traceability of stock movement
- Reduce manual compliance errors

---

## Positioning Note

Pharmaloop does not override regulatory frameworks; it enhances operational compliance through better data and visibility.
