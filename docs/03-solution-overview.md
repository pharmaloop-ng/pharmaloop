# Solution Overview

Pharmaloop introduces a unified digital infrastructure for pharmacy operations with a strong focus on climate resilience, supply chain intelligence, and equitable access to essential medicines, particularly for children and vulnerable populations in climate-exposed communities.

## Core Modules

### 1. Pharmacy Operations System
- Inventory tracking
- Sales monitoring
- Stock alerts
- Expiry management

---

### 2. Climate Intelligence Layer
- Temperature risk awareness
- Storage condition insights
- Wastage risk prediction

---

### 3. Supply Chain Network
- Distributor coordination
- Supplier visibility
- Order optimization

---

### 4. Compliance System
- Digital record keeping
- Audit readiness
- Regulatory tracking support

---

## Key Differentiator

Unlike traditional pharmacy software, Pharmaloop integrates **climate conditions as a core operational variable**, not an afterthought.

---

## Result

A more efficient, resilient, and data-driven pharmacy ecosystem.
